### 8832-r1-i06  `backend/internal/service/session/workspace_file_paths.go:143`

Whichever you pick, the paths read in the non-truncated branch need it too. The fallback in `fetchWorkspaceFilePaths` does filter deleted, so the two routes now disagree.
