## 5. Observations

- #29308 is the clearest escape in the batch: #16961 got a Macroscope Critical and a CodeRabbit Major on the same two lines, nobody replied, and the defect was fixed a month later by an outside contributor. Two bots agreeing on a top-severity data-loss finding was not enough to make the team act on a 35-file PR.
- #31337 shows CodeRabbit at its best: three Major threads, each a real data-loss path in secret handling, each fixed with a failing-then-passing test. The author (an AI-assisted loop) replied to every thread; in this chunk that reply habit decides whether findings get fixed.
- CodeRabbit severity is again inflated and often retracted: 3 Major threads (#30467, #29678 T1, #29832 T2) and one Minor (#29150) ended with the bot withdrawing or the author declining, usually after the author ran something the bot only inferred.
- Macroscope left only 3 threads here, two of them test-hygiene suggestions ("fold this test into the existing case") and one real scroll-retry bug on #31286; its `Test Signal` category is common and low value.
- Escapes: 6 of 9 resolved cases were missed by both bots at introduction, mostly on large refactor or feature PRs (#18505, #25733, #4849, #7260). The two caught-and-ignored cases (#16961, #29643) were both flagged correctly and deferred as out of scope or unanswered.
