## 5. Observations

- Macroscope produced all 3 real High findings that were fixed before merge (#33565 T3, #33511, #33503), each verified in code, and each by reasoning about ordering ("tag before submit", "timestamp before success", "save after remove"). CodeRabbit's single real one (#33565 T4) was a security hardening.
- Both bots ran well on small, fast PRs: of 12 threads, 8 were fixed before merge, mostly because the author (often an AI-assisted loop) replied to each and named the fixing commit.
- Escapes were again domain-semantic or environment-semantic (single-executable bundling, Chromium `:focus-visible`, scale on a 47 MB thread). #33093 drew about 18 bot comments and none touched the bundler.
- Bot severity labels are inflated here too: CodeRabbit "Major" on #33543 T2 was a test-mock failure (sev 1) and on #33538 a declined scope request (sev 2). Macroscope's "High" on #33565 T2 was simply wrong (temporary workflow).
- #33501 is a clean "caught, deferred, shipped, fixed in 2 days" case: Macroscope named the stale-inset bug on #33229 and the maintainer left it open (unverified whether deliberate triage).
