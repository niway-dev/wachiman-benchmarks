- Bots left only 8 threads on 25 PRs. Both High/Major real bugs (#32231, #32446) were race or lifetime bugs inside the fix's own first version and both bots (or Macroscope alone) found them before merge.
- On #32231 CodeRabbit and Macroscope reported the same race independently on the same commit; CodeRabbit's extra Minor (retry policy) was declined by the author with a one-line reason and the bot accepted it.
- CodeRabbit's three #32269 threads are docs-wording nits on a docs-only PR; all were left unresolved and the PR merged.
- Every escaped bug traced here was missed at introduction; the intro PRs were large (#9348 169 files, #28842 213 files, #12536 23 commits) and the bugs were omissions (a missing step, a deleted behaviour, a missing alias), which line-level bots rarely flag.
- Stack branches hide history: two of the regressions (#32248, #32249) came from an open, unmerged layer-4 PR, so SZZ on main returns unrelated later refactors and #14290/#25233; only the fix PR's body names the real origin.
