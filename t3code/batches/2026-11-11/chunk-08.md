## 5. Observations

- Humans beat both bots on the root causes: no bot caught the defect in any of the 15 introducing PRs checked for the defect the fix later removed. The nearest misses were adjacent comments in the same file (#17496, #23957, #27958, #22253).
- Authors refuted 12 threads with code-level arguments and CodeRabbit withdrew after checking (#19665, #18588, #18442, #15848); Macroscope never answered, it answered author replies seen here with "unable to act", so its disputed threads stay open.
- Severity labels are poorly calibrated: CodeRabbit "Major" threads were wrong or real-minor (#21661 T2, #18588 T1/T2, #10495 T1, #14170 T1), while "Minor" threads were stuck-service defects (#19652 T3, #15255 T1/T2).
- Concurrency and ordering windows were the strongest bot contribution in this chunk (#15848, #15255, #21239, #19652), each fixed before merge, but five of those threads cite windows the author could not reproduce live.
- Macroscope reviews 150-file feature PRs with 30+ comments mostly on Medium style and edge cases, and none touched the later bug (#4860, #6039, #5181), so volume did not translate into recall on the introducing PR.
