## 5. Observations on bot behaviour

- This chunk is one author (juliusmarminge) with agent-written fix PRs that each carry a reproduction. Bot threads on them are mostly test, wording and docs points: 9 of 27 are nits and 2 were withdrawn by the bot after the author's evidence (CR accepted both and resolved the thread).
- CodeRabbit labelled Major on 4 threads (#26625 T1/T2, #26322 T1, #25911 T1); one was withdrawn, one was a test-fixture break, one was a real edge, one is plausible but unproven. Macroscope's one High (#26625 T4) was real but small and was fixed without the thread being resolved.
- The bots almost never flagged the defects that later needed a fix PR: 15 of 17 traced introductions had no bot comment on the defect, and 3 of the introductions were #2829, where about 800 comments still missed all five later bugs. Volume is not coverage.
- The one clean catch (#20413, `~` expansion) was argued down by the author and withdrawn by CodeRabbit; the fix PR about three weeks later proved the first comment right. A bot that backs off after one reply loses a true finding.
- Bots did not run on #3155 and #3099 (0 comments), the two oldest introducing PRs in this chunk. Pre-bot history cannot be replayed against these reviewers.
