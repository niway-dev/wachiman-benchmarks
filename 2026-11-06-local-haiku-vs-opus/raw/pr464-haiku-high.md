## Findings

### 🟡🐛 **Consider · bug: Library ready card's Show in Finder and drag leave the copy unseen (confidence medium)**

`apps/kaipu-record/src/renderer/src/features/library/components/ready-copy-card.tsx:80`

The Library's Ready to upload card does not mark the copy seen when the user runs Show in Finder (ready-copy-card.tsx:80) or drags it out (ready-copy-card.tsx:90-94). The status card's same two actions do call markCopySeen (export-status-cards.tsx:280-281 and 293-294), and ready-copies.ts:9-11 says acting on the ready card clears the unseen state. As written, after the user has revealed or dragged the file from the Library card, the 'N copies ready' chip and the outline stay on that copy when they come back. Either call markCopySeen in both handlers, or change the ready-copies.ts comment to say the Library card's actions do not clear it.

### 🟡🐛 **Consider · bug: A leading copy card drops the tag, Send to, edit and delete actions (confidence high)**

`apps/kaipu-record/src/renderer/src/pages/library/library-page.tsx:409`

While a copy leads the grid, its ReadyCopyCard replaces the regular VideoCard, but ReadyCopyCard receives no onDelete, onTags, onPrepare or onEdit (ready-copy-card.tsx:11-19). The regular card gets all four at library-page.tsx:425-443. So a just-finished copy cannot be tagged, sent to Prepare, opened in the editor or deleted from the grid until its card is closed or the user leaves the Library. Consider passing the same handlers, or saying so in the leading-card comment at library-page.tsx:123-125.

### 🟡🐛 **Consider · bug: Pill's progressbar is nested in a button and loses its role (confidence medium)**

`apps/kaipu-record/src/renderer/src/features/status-cards/export-status-cards.tsx:216`

The minimized pill puts a role="progressbar" span inside a <button aria-label=...> (export-status-cards.tsx:199-222). Button children are presentational in ARIA, so assistive tech does not expose the progressbar or its aria-valuenow, and the button's own label replaces the progress text. The expanded card's track (lines 156-163) keeps the role. Consider making the pill a container with its own accessible name, with the expand control separate, so the progress value stays exposed.

### 🟡📝 **Consider · docs: ExportJobInfo.destination doc quotes a card title that no longer exists (confidence high)**

The cards now read 'Exporting for YouTube' and 'Exporting your video' (en.json:1413 progressTitle, used at export-status-cards.tsx:141). Update the example to match.

### 🟡❓ **Consider · question: Library ready card's Show in Finder and drag do not mark the copy seen (confidence high)**
