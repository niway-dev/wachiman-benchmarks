- E2 is partial for W31 and W32, yes for W33. In W31 and W32 the only mention of the stale comment is one sentence inside the E6 `Fix` comment, the treatment W5 and W6 got in the earlier analyses; W33 posted a separate `Consider · docs` on `recorder-store.ts:938`.

- E6 is yes for W31 even though the comment is anchored at `recorder-store.ts:928` (the off branch). The body names the queued on reading `cameraWanted === false`, opening the camera while `webcamOn = false`, and the early return on `!webcamOn`. Its title ("an off that follows a queued on is undone") is the same defect.
- W33 has no E6: its `recorder-store.ts:943` comment is A2 (a superseded open misread as "camera is back"), a different defect on a different branch of the same code, and nothing in W33 names the missing `webcamOn` check.
- W33 N3 is partial, not yes. It states the mechanism (the live-toggle queue is held for up to 6 s and a mic or system-audio toggle waits behind it) but names only two toggles and carries it as a clause of the N1 comment; W12 and W15 named the full set and counted as yes, W10's single closing sentence as partial.
- W31 N5 is partial: the question names the Chromium-only dependency but not the silent fallback to the old behaviour; W32 states the fallback and counts as yes (still no word on whether it is logged).
- The W31 and W32 `webcam-relay.ts:1` questions and the W33 `recorder-engine.ts:709` question are counted as findings, like W15's body question; they are open questions about library and OS behaviour, so their verdicts stay "unverified" as for N2 and N4 in earlier files, not invalid.
- N7 (W32) is valid on the code but lies outside the PR's diff; counted as a finding because the review posted it and it is a real inconsistency the PR's own spec edit created.
- The W31 "What the change connects to" entry mentions E1's failed-reopen limitation ("same best-effort limitation as an unplugged camera") but as a closed note, not a finding: E1 stays "no" for all three runs.
