## Judgement calls

- E4 W31 is "yes": it names the mechanism (30 px offset plus 80 px blur against 16 px of padding, `hasShadow: false`, transparent window) at the line that sets the padding. It does not quote the claim in the comment at `main.css:82-83`, and it hedges ("may clip", low confidence, "check this on a real display"); E4 is a mechanism, and W31 states it. First run of any version to post it.
- E1 W31 and W33 are "no", on the W13 precedent: the failure to load is named only as a limit the PR already acknowledges, inside "What the change connects to", with no finding and no mention of `show()` answering true. E1 W32 is "yes" although it does not name `did-fail-load` or `render-process-gone`, on the W15 and focus-replay W12 precedent (dropped promise or answer-before-paint named, timeout proposed).
- N1 W31 is "no" although W31 proposes closing the card when a take starts: the root (nothing closes the card at take start) is shared, but the finding is about the main window being shown into the take (NF1 in the v1.4.0 analysis), and that analysis kept NF1 and NF2 as separate rows. It is counted as N8. A reader who groups by root cause would call it "partial".
- N1 W33 is "yes" at `main/index.ts:897` instead of `recording-ready-window.ts:208`: same missing dismissal before a screenshot or the next take, with the same "content protection alone" consequence; it cites `:208` in the body of the comment.
- The W32 body questions at `recording-ready-window.ts:134` and `use-floating-ready-card.ts:18` are counted as posted findings (8 for W32) but are duplicates of its inline comments; by distinct defect W32 posted 6.
- N7 W33 is mapped to N7, not to a new row: both ask whether `focusable: true` (for Escape) is acceptable, a decision rather than a defect, with different platform worries.
- N4 W32 is "yes" although it says `probeSource` never throws (`probe-source.ts:42`); the focus-replay analysis found `openRecordingInput` is outside that `try` (`probe-source.ts:29`). The finding's point, a stalled probe leaves no card, floating or in-app, is the N4 consequence.
- A2 is "no" in W31 and W33: their `post-recording-card.tsx:47` comment concerns the line's length, not the claim that the card stays until the user "leaves the page" (A2); filed as N9.
