- N1 W13 and W15 are "no": W13's "What the reviewer checked" says the page-level Enter handlers leave the picker's dialog alone and W15's says the Enter guards are fine because picker items are buttons; neither posts the finding.
- A6 stays "no" for W15: its batch 3 note that stale doc mentions of the deleted files "sit in other parts' files, so they are not reported" is an aside, not a finding on the leftover docs row or the i18n key.
- A1 W13 is "yes" although posted as `Fix · question`: it states the mechanism (Chromium's default navigates to a dropped file, no `will-navigate` guard in main) and asks only whether it is true.
- W7–W9 cells of N6–N10 are "no": those rows did not exist when W7–W9 were labelled, and no W7–W9 finding in the v1.20.1 analysis was mapped to the same concern.
