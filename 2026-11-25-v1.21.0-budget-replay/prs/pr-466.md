## Judgement calls

- E1 W15 is "yes" although it is a body question, not an inline comment: it states that `show` answers true before the card can paint and that a renderer that never loads leaves the user with no card and no fallback, which is the E1 consequence. W12 of the focus replay was counted "yes" on the same grounds (a timeout proposed, the dropped promise and listeners not named).
- E1 W13 is "no": its aside names the consequence only as an author-acknowledged limit and neither says that `show()` answers true nor lists a fix.
- W14 `recording-ready-window.ts:171` and W15 `main/index.ts:899` are N2 (v1.20.1), not new: W8's finding was that deleting the recording leaves a card whose actions target a missing asset, which is what both say (they add that the toast's hand-off checks the vault, `index.ts:683-694`).
- W14 `recording-ready-window.ts:208` is N1 (v1.20.1) at the same line, with the same missing hide-before-capture hook; its Linux framing is an extra reason, not a different bug.
- W15 `library-detail-page.tsx:140` (body question) is N4: it asks about a later card and no card when the probe never settles, with no inline twin in that run, so it is not a duplicate.
- W7–W9 cells of N3–N5 are "no": those rows did not exist when W7–W9 were labelled, and no W7–W9 finding in the v1.20.1 analysis was mapped to the same concern.
