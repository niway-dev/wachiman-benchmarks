👮 **Wachiman** · 🟡🐛 **Consider · bug: Double-clicking a name to select it still toggles the row once (confidence medium)**

The guard reads the selection when the click fires. A drag-select is covered: the selection exists by the time mouseup sends the click. A double-click on a word in `.rowNow` or `.rowAfter`, the usual way to grab a name, sends two clicks. On the first one nothing is selected yet, because Chromium selects the word on the second mousedown. So the first click toggles the row and only the second is skipped, and the row ends up flipped after the copy. Triple-click behaves the same way. One way to close this is to skip the toggle when `event.detail > 1`, though that alone doesn't undo the first click's toggle. Another is to toggle on the first click and toggle back on `onDoubleClick`. Or you can accept this edge case, since the dimmed row shows the change.
