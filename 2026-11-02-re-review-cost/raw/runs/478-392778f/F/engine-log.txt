--- apps/kaipu-record/src/main/workspaces/registry.ts:634 ---
👮 **Wachiman** · 🟡🐛 **Consider · bug: An offline rename of a pendingInit workspace is now lost if its folder comes back with an identity from another Mac (confidence medium)**

`edit` now keeps `pendingInit` when the folder could not be written (line 634), and sets `pendingIdentity` (line 638). Suppose the folder later comes back holding an `identity.json` with an id this Mac doesn't know. `availability` then takes the adoption branch (lines 718-727): `Object.assign(entry, entryFromIdentity(...))` replaces the name, color and initials with the folder's values, and `pendingIdentity` is never consulted. The user's rename is gone, and nothing tells them. Before this change `pendingInit` was cleared, so the same folder was reported as `different` instead. This takes a drive that was away at upgrade, a rename while it was away, and another Mac writing an identity in the meantime, which is the follow-up you already noted on the earlier thread. If you want to keep the rename, the adoption branch could re-apply `name`/`color`/`initials` and keep `pendingIdentity` when the entry has it, so the next probe writes them to the file.
