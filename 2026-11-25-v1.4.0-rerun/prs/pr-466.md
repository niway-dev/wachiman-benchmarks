## Judgement calls

- W18 `recording-ready-window.ts:146` -> E1 (full): it states that `show()` answers true before the card can paint, that nothing handles `did-fail-load` or `render-process-gone`, and that the page therefore hides its in-app fallback so the card is lost with no fallback. This is more complete than W15 of the budget replay (a body question that did not name the missing listeners), and an inline comment.
- W16 `recording-ready-window.ts:206` and W17 `recording-ready-window.ts:208` -> N1 (v1.20.1): the finding is that nothing closes the card before the next take or screenshot, as W7's; the Windows-before-19041 and Linux framing is the reason given, as in W14 of the budget replay. The v1.4.0 analysis called the same defect NF2 (W5, W6); that ID is not carried into the replay docs.
- W17 `library-detail-page.tsx:124` -> N2 (v1.20.1): the card has no tie to the page and survives leaving it or deleting the recording, which is the N2 defect; it adds that the chips then lead to a "not found" page.
- W16 `library-detail-page.tsx:142` and W17 `library-detail-page.tsx:140` -> N4 (v1.21.0 focus): a probe that never settles leaves no card, floating or in-app. The v1.4.0 analysis called it NF4 (W4) and judged it unconfirmed; N4 is the row that holds it here.
- W18's batch 1 summary item "Silent loss when the card renderer never loads" is the E1 comment above; the review body repeats an inline finding, not a second finding.
- E4 is "no" for all three: W18's batch 2 summary repeats the padding claim; no run examined the shadow clip.
- W7–W9 cells of N3–N7 are "no": those rows did not exist when W7–W9 were labelled, and no W7–W9 finding in the v1.20.1 analysis was mapped to the same concern.
