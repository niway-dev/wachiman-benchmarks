## Judgement calls

- W17 `webcam-model.test.ts:105` -> E5 (full) although the comment is anchored on the test file: it names `webcam-model.ts:155`, the hash over the segment ids (`export-reuse.ts:81-83`) and the consequence (a second Share re-encodes), which is the E5 defect and mechanism. Its own caveat (it did not read `stableStringify`) is in the review body; the head shows `wholeFingerprint` hashes `stableStringify(webcam)`.
- E2 is "partial" for W16 and W18 under the rule the v1.4.0 analysis used for W5 and W6: the point (the comment at 938 describes a queued off that does not exist) is made in one sentence inside a `Fix` comment about another defect, not as its own finding.
- W16 `recorder-store.ts:943` is A2, not E6: its sequence (on, off, on, off... with the first on still opening) ends with the superseded on returning "ok" and closing the gap early, which is the A2 mechanism; it does not say a queued on opens the camera while the toggle shows off.
- W18 `recorder-store.ts:939` is E6 (full): it names the queued callback that never checks `webcamOn`, the camera opening after a later off, the light and the face in the webcam file, and proposes the `if (!webcamOn) return;` check. The mic-queue ordering it uses as one example (a camera-on queued behind a mic change) is the reverse of N3 (a mic mute waiting behind a camera reopen), so N3 stays "no".
- N2 (v1.20.1) stays "no" for W17: its open question on mediabunny (`frameRate`, `sizeChangeBehavior`, repeating the last frame) is in "What the change connects to", not a posted comment, and the v1.4.0 analysis left the same open point unposted.
- N1 (v1.20.1) stays "no" for W18: its mention of a camera that "reopens ... after a toggle made while a mic change is still opening its device" is the E6 mechanism, not the doubled reopen timeout.
- W7–W9 cells of N4 and N5 are "no": those rows did not exist when W7–W9 were labelled, and no W7–W9 finding in the v1.20.1 analysis was mapped to the same concern.
